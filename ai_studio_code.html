Common Questions

Does this replace my CA or accountant?

+

No. MSME Twin works alongside your CA. While your CA looks backward to file returns and audit taxes, MSME Twin looks forward 90 days to ensure you actually have the liquid cash in your account when the tax is due.

What if I have multiple current accounts?

+

Our Account Aggregator integration can sync multiple FIPs (Financial Information Providers) simultaneously, merging all your cash flows into one holistic digital twin.

Do I have to upload invoices manually?

+

Never. By connecting with the GSTN portal via API, we automatically pull your B2B outward supplies (GSTR-1) and e-Way bills and match them against your bank inflows.
